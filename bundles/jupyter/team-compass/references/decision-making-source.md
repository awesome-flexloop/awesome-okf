## docs/team/decision-making.md 核心内容

Jupyter Server 团队遵循 Jupyter 主治理文档中描述的["Decision Making" Guidelines](https://jupyter.org/governance/decision_making.html#required-aspects-of-decision-making)。

### 决策流程

1. **非正式共识优先**：首先寻求非正式共识
2. **投票兜底**：如果无法达成明确共识，活跃成员可以发起投票
3. 投票流程遵循 [Jupyter Governance model](https://jupyter.org/governance/decision_making.html#required-aspects-of-decision-making) 中的指南

### 团队规模

团队规模没有上限。遵循 Jupyter 更广泛治理模型中[bootstrap决策机构指南](https://jupyter.org/governance/bootstrapping_decision_making.html#bootstrapping-decision-making-bodies)的原则：

> The new governance model and decision-making guide is designed to support large, highly participatory decision-making bodies. As such, even Subprojects that have a clear decision-making body today may wish to increase the size of that body to include more contributors.
>
> （新治理模型和决策指南旨在支持大型、高参与度的决策机构。因此，即使是今天已有明确决策机构的子项目，也可能希望扩大该机构的规模以纳入更多贡献者。）
